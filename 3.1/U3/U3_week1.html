Word Study — Prefixes
Prefixes are added to the beginning of base words. Knowing the meaning of a prefix can help you figure out the meaning of a word.
pre-
means "before"
preapprovepreviewprepay
dis-
means "not"
disagreedisappear
in-
means "in," "on," or "not"
indirectinsecureincorrectinaccurateinformal
im-
means "into" or "not"
imperfectimpoliteimmatureimmobile
non-
means "not"
nonfictionnonstop
Tip: im- is used before words starting with m or p (immature, impolite). in- is used before most other letters (incorrect, informal).
Language & Conventions — Subject-Verb Agreement
The subject and the verb in a sentence must work together, or agree.
  • To make most present tense verbs agree with singular subjects, add -s.
  • If the subject is a plural noun or pronoun, the present tense verb usually does not end with -s.
NounPronounSingular or Plural
Billy plays guitar.He plays guitar.singular
The girl runs.She runs.singular
The dogs chase the ball.They chase the ball.plural
The boys laugh.They laugh.plural
The verb "be": is / am / are (present) · was / were (past)
I am · He, She, It is · You, We, They are
