Grammar: Plural Possessive Nouns

▾
A plural possessive noun shows that two or more people share or own something.
Rule 1: Plural nouns ending in -s, -es, -ies → just add ' (apostrophe only)
Rule 2: Irregular plural nouns (not ending in -s) → add 's
Noun (Plural)Plural PossessiveExample
scientistsscientists'the scientists' boots
wolveswolves'the wolves' den
puppiespuppies'the puppies' mother
children (irregular)children'sthe children's backpacks
men (irregular)men'sthe men's hats
women (irregular)women'sthe women's team
people (irregular)people'sthe people's ideas
geese (irregular)geese'sthe geese's nests

Spelling: Syllable Pattern VCe

▾
VCe pattern: Vowel + Consonant + silent e → the vowel says its long sound.
Patterns: a_e (make) · i_e (time) · e_e (these) · o_e (hope) · u_e (use)
