Spelling: r-Controlled Vowels (ar, or, ore, oar)

▾
When a vowel is followed by r, it makes a special sound:
ar → /ar/ (alarm, dark)   or → /or/ (format, morning)
ore → /or/ (adore, explore)   oar → /or/ (soar, board)

Vocabulary & High-Frequency Words

▾
alarm 鬧鐘
(n) a device that makes a sound to get attention
darkness 黑暗
(n) the state of not having any light
target 目標
(n) goal or aim; something you want to achieve
absorb 吸收
(v) to take in water, liquid, or another substance
format 模板；格式
(n) the way something is arranged; (v) to get ready to be used
morning 早晨
(n) the time from sunrise to noon
adore 愛慕
(v) to admire someone a lot; to love something very much
explore 探索
(v) to go to a place and learn about it
cardboard 硬紙板
(n) thick, stiff paper used for making boxes
soar 翱翔
(v) to fly very high; to rise quickly
finally 最後
(adv) after a long time or some difficulty; at the end
money 金錢
(n) that people use to buy things
