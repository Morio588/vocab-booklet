Grammar: Singular Possessive Nouns

▾
A singular possessive noun shows that one person, place, or thing owns something.
Rule: add apostrophe + s ( 's ) to a singular noun.
NounSingular PossessiveExample
doctordoctor'sthe doctor's office
babybaby'sthe baby's toy
dogdog'sthe dog's bone
RayRay'sRay's computer

Spelling: Compound Words

▾
Compound words are made by joining two smaller words together to create a new word with a new meaning.
e.g. air + port = airport

Vocabulary & High-Frequency Words

▾
airport 機場
(n) a place where planes take off and land
eyesight 視力
(n) the ability to see; the range or clarity of vision
fireworks 煙火
(n) bright, colorful explosions in the sky used for celebration
football 足球/橄欖球
(n) a game played with a ball that is kicked or carried to score points
haircut 理髮
(n) the act of cutting hair; the style of someone's hair
moonlight 月光
(n) the light that comes from the moon
outside 室外
(adv) the area not inside a building; not part of a group
playground 操場
(n) a place with equipment for children to play
popcorn 爆米花
(n) corn that pops when heated, usually eaten at movies or parties
rattlesnake 響尾蛇
(n) a type of snake that makes a rattling sound with its tail
minute 分鐘
(n) a unit of time equal to 60 seconds; a short moment of time
decide 決定
(v) to make a choice
